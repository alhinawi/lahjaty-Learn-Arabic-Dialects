# Fujairah coastline note

25 November 2026

## Correction

The existing `emi/famous.fujairah` note called Fujairah the only emirate on the Gulf of Oman. Official sources distinguish being entirely on the eastern coast from having any coast on the gulf. Sharjah also has Gulf of Oman coastline.

The revised Arabic and English note keeps that distinction explicit. It drops the separate mountain/seascape flourish rather than leaving it without a citation.

## Sources read

- UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, The UAE, Fujairah section: https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/the-uae . Describes Fujairah as entirely on the eastern coast along the Gulf of Oman.
- Official UAE Government platform, Sharjah, Location and Geography: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/the-seven-emirates/Sharjah . Describes Sharjah as lying on both coasts, including the Gulf of Oman in the east.
- Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security, Fujairah Customs: https://icp.gov.ae/en/fujairah-customs/ . Cross-checks Fujairah coastline solely on the Gulf of Oman, not the Arabian Gulf. Page shows last updated8January2024. Not a third product citation.

No area, population, coastline length, border, port superlative or tourism ranking is adopted.
