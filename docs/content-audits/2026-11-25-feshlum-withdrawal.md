# Disputed Feshlum dish entry withdrawn

25 November 2026

The Benghazi `famous.feshlum` entry taught فشلوم as Fishlum, a spicy fish dish, with Standard Arabic طبق سمك. Its note instead described a seaside city and its example did not contain the headword. No fetched source established the purported dish meaning or Benghazi attribution.

## Decision

Withdraw this entry from the learning corpus, search, daily selection and generated social cards. Preserve its old shared URL as an explicit bilingual correction page. Do not invent a substitute dish, move the old example to Tripoli, claim that the term can never have another use, or recast a neighborhood name as a distinctive dialect expression. Existing saved favorites/progress are not deleted or rewritten. The invalid saved favorite is filtered by the existing favorites view.

The previous record remains available in git history. Famous entries decrease by one,936to935; this is deliberate accuracy work, not lost expansion. Other words and recordings are unchanged.
