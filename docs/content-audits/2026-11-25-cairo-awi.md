# Cairo awi usage note

25 November 2026

## Scope

The Cairo `famous.awi` note said that Q turns into a glottal stop and that the entry always follows an adjective. Replace this with a narrower usage note: awi means very or so much, can follow an adjective, and can also intensify a verb. Do not turn this entry into a general pronunciation rule.

## Sources read

- Lisaan Masry, Egyptian Arabic grammar, adjectives: https://www.lisaanmasry.org/grammar/adjectives.html . Its degree-adverb section places awi after adjectives and gives adjective examples.
- Desert Sky, Adverbs in Egyptian Arabic: https://www.arabic.desert-sky.net/g_adv.html . Its degree-adverb section gives both an adjective example and a liking-the-film verb example for awi. This directly defeats the adjective-only claim.
- Wiktionary, قوي, Egyptian Arabic section: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%D9%82%D9%88%D9%8A . Cross-check only for adverb meaning and alternate spelling; regional pronunciation and etymology not adopted.

The two teaching pages are cited in the entry. They support this restricted usage note, not every dialect form on the site. No universal pronunciation, frequency or historical claim is added.
