# Gaza and keffiyeh context review

Reviewed November 22, 2026. Two existing rural Palestinian entries, bilingual notes only. Headwords, examples and entry counts unchanged.

## Gaza

Replaced the unsourced comparison of the port's age with a basic Mediterranean-coast location. This does not deny Gaza's ancient maritime history. It avoids conflating the city, its present harbour and historical Anthedon or Maiumas sites, or turning the age of one site into a Mediterranean-wide ranking.

The Historic Centre of Gaza submission locates the city on the Mediterranean coast. Both that record and Anthedon's record are State Party submissions on the Tentative List, not adopted World Heritage inscriptions or UNESCO verification of every historical claim. Anthedon's 2012 condition descriptions were not reused as current conditions. UNESCO's dated 2026 damage assessment was also checked to avoid implying that old preservation or access descriptions remain current. No damage count, access advice, visit recommendation or claim of intact monuments was added.

## Keffiyeh

The old Arabic note claimed centuries of weaving in Hebron while the English was less specific. The replacement retains the identity context and gives a supported example: the Hirbawi factory in Hebron and its 1961 start. This date belongs to that factory, not the origin of the keffiyeh or all weaving in Hebron. No claim is made that earlier weaving did not exist.

The factory's own shop states its history, its official distribution page identifies the Hebron location, and a June 12, 2021 Heinrich Boll Foundation profile independently reports the 1961 opening and Palestinian identity context. The shop's relationship to the factory is corroborated by the factory's own official page. No last-remaining-factory, production-volume, product-authenticity or purchasing claim was reused.
