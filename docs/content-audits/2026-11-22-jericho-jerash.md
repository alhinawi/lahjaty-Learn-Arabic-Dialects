# Jericho and Jerash place-note review

Reviewed November 22, 2026. Two existing entries retained; only their bilingual notes changed. Headwords, examples and counts are unchanged.

## Jericho

Replaced the absolute oldest-city claim with the precise relationship between present-day Jericho and the UNESCO-listed archaeological site of Ancient Jericho/Tell es-Sultan to its northwest. This avoids treating a modern city and a particular archaeological property as interchangeable. Removed the unsourced banana identity and elevation context from this note rather than widening the patch.

UNESCO's current listing and adopted 2023 statement support the site's location and inscription. The ICOMOS 2023 evaluation, in its discussion of criterion (iii), explicitly questions confirmation of the oldest-fortified-city claim and notes gaps in occupation. Therefore no oldest-city or continuous-occupation ranking is retained, and the note does not substitute a different ranking.

Fetched and read November 22, 2026:
- UNESCO listing: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1687/
- Adopted decision 45 COM 8B.36: https://whc.unesco.org/en/decisions/8417/
- ICOMOS evaluation: https://whc.unesco.org/document/205764

The adopted statement and expert evaluation were distinguished from nomination claims. Being hosted by UNESCO does not mean every claim in a nomination was adopted.

## Jerash

The previous Arabic note implied a complete Roman city and used an Italy-based comparison; the English note described preservation more cautiously. The replacement uses northern Jordan, the ancient name Gerasa, colonnaded streets and Roman-era theatres in both languages. The tourism sources support a high-preservation description, but that is not the same as an intact or complete city. No preservation ranking, travel time, festival date or admission information was copied into the note.

## Limits

This corrects place context, not dialect accuracy. Existing spellings, vowels, translations and example sentences are not independently validated by these sources. The unchanged banana example is illustrative praise, not a sourced ranking; the unchanged festival example is not a checked current event schedule. No audio or native-speaker-review claim. Prior Palestinian and Sharqia lexical caveats remain in force. No numerical expansion or hosting changes.
