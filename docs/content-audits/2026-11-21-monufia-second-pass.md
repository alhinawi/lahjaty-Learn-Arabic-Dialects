# Monufia factual review, second pass

Reviewed November 21, 2026. Four existing entries revised, no entries added or removed.

## Mit Abu El-Kom

The previous entry assigned both Sadat and Mubarak's birthplace to this village. SIS gives Sadat's birthplace as Mit Abu El-Kom in Monufia. Ahram Online gives Mubarak's birthplace as Kafr Meselha. The note and example now mention only Sadat's supported birthplace.

Sources fetched and read:

- SIS, Anwar al-Sadat biography, February 16, 2016: https://sis.gov.eg/Story/99387?lang=en-us
- Ahram Online, Mubarak timeline, February 25, 2020: https://english.ahram.org.eg/NewsContentP/1/364123/Egypt/Mubarak-A-timeline.aspx

## Sers El-Layan

The previous note called it a Pharaonic village with an open-air museum and used a statue example. Those claims were not supported by the official city overview. The replacement gives only the city location between Menouf and El-Bagour districts, directly supported by the governorate page. Absence from that overview is not proof that no antiquities exist; the change removes unverified specificity.

Official governorate overview, displayed November 12, 2024, fetched and read:
http://www.monofeya.gov.eg/HaykalTanzemy/cities/sers/disp_hawla_markaz.aspx?ID=1
