The Saidi `famous.luxor` note claimed that Luxor holds a third of the world's antiquities. - UNESCO World Heritage Centre, Ancient Thebes with its Necropolis: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/87/ . Describes the listed property, including Karnak and Luxor temples and the Valley of the Kings. Used for heritage scope, not every feature or building of modern Luxor.
- Egyptian Ministry of Tourism and Antiquities, Ancient Thebes and its Necropolis: https://egymonuments.gov.eg/en/world-heritage/ancient-thebes-and-its-necropolis . Identifies ancient Thebes as modern Luxor, names the monuments and the listed property. No prices, opening hours or visitor logistics adopted.
