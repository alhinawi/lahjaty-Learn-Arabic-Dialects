# Monufia location corrections

Reviewed November 21, 2026. Scope: two `mnf` entries in `src/content/famous.json`.

## Birket El-Sab

The Monufia governorate city page places Birket El-Sab in northeastern Monufia, on the Cairo-Alexandria agricultural road. The previous entry incorrectly placed it in western Monufia and included unsupported claims about the Rosetta branch and Khatatba. Those claims are replaced with the governorate-level location.

Source, fetched November 21, 2026:
http://www.monofeya.gov.eg/HaykalTanzemy/cities/bert_esabal/disp_hawla_markaz.aspx?ID=2

## Tala

The governorate city page places Tala in northern Monufia, with Birket El-Sab district to its east. The previous entry said southern Monufia. It also associated Tala with Louis Awad; the State Information Service biography gives his birthplace as Sharona, Maghagha, Minya. The biography claim and unverified canal example are removed, not replaced with another unsourced detail.

Sources, fetched November 21, 2026:
http://www.monofeya.gov.eg/HaykalTanzemy/cities/tala/Dispabout_markaz.aspx
https://sis.gov.eg/Story/26529/Louis-Awad

## Limits

These are factual location corrections. They do not validate any dialect-specific pronunciation or establish that place names are distinctive vocabulary. No entries are added or removed. Other content still needs source review. Code tests and a successful build do not establish linguistic or factual accuracy.
